About Our Project

Learn more about Household Donation Exchange.

Our Mission

The main purpose of Household Donation Exchange is to connect people who have unused household items with people who need those items.

Our website encourages donation, reuse, environmental awareness and community support.

Our Vision

We aim to create a community where useful household items are shared instead of being wasted.

Problem Statement

Many household items are discarded even when they are still useful. People who need these items often have difficulty finding affordable or free resources nearby.

There is a need for a simple and organized platform where donors and recipients can connect.

Project Objectives

  • Encourage household item donations.
  • Reduce waste by promoting reuse.
  • Connect donors with recipients.
  • Provide an organized donation platform.
  • Support community cooperation.
  • Promote environmental sustainability.
